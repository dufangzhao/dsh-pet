# Style-range gate

The user rejected five layouts of replica controls, then five layouts of one original kit. This task counts independent style and widget families.
Five candidates: retained labelled Workbench, plus new Precision, Hearth, Folio and Prism. Pairwise difference must cover several visual dimensions and at least two meaningful control structures or interactions. A palette, radius or grid swap is insufficient.
Each candidate owns its complete window frame, actual controls and a component/state board. The neutral selector is review infrastructure; it must not wrap all candidates in the unchanged Workbench sidebar.
Share StartModel, theme/gesture/expression controllers, raw owned character and existing config/credentials/history/capture authorities. Four new families do not render the existing Workbench kit/Preview/Studio markup as their actual UI.
Family default colors are presentation seeds in the isolated review. Simply switching a candidate does not persist user settings or clear model/Key drafts. Explicit actions retain real effects and receipts.
Review actual frame and close controls side by side before counting the result. Existing layout variants remain historical or secondary, not evidence of five styles.
