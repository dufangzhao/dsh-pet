## Review method

Compare whole windows plus close views of actual controls against the rejected screenshots atqa/screenshots/start-design-lab/start-lab-1793272859631-b3d0f795. Inspect ordinary/focused/disabled/pending/error/selected states on the original component board. The board is labelled design samples; it is not a fake connected backend.

No marker/class rename, source absence grep, layout movement or palette replacement alone passes originality. Real functional tests must also pass. NativeHTML semantics and actiontestids are reusable instrumentation, not the rejected visual component. Data/controllers/Core/credentials/recording/theme-store remain single owners.

Original builtin theme migration must preserve custom schemes and savedmode. The new brand may define new builtin values; it must not discard customer-authored colors or private data. Other settings-page bodies and desktop bubble redesign remain later scope, while sharedshell andStart controls are this scope.
